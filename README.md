# Northwind-Traders-Analysis

Client Brief: Northwind Traders AnalysisYou have been asked to analyze the Northwind database and deliver insights that can help management make decisions.Deliverable: A short report (with key findings + supporting queries/code) answering the questions below.Questions to Answer
Customer Value
Who are our top 10 customers by total revenue? How much of our total revenue do they represent?
Product Performance
Which products generate the most revenue, and which ones are underperforming (low or zero sales)?
Category Analysis
Which product categories drive the most revenue? Are there categories with high quantity but low revenue (or vice versa)?
Employee Performance
Which employees are generating the highest sales? Is there a big gap between the top and bottom performers?
Order Patterns
What is the average order value? Are there customers who place many small orders vs. few large orders?
Time Trends (if order dates are available)
How have monthly sales trended? Are there any noticeable peaks or drops?
Opportunity / Risk
Based on the data, what is one clear opportunity and one potential risk for the business?
